### Priority Levels
- **CRITICAL**: Complete production outage, data loss risk, severe security incident, or broad user impact.
- **HIGH**: Significant performance degradation, core feature failure with no workaround, or active service outage.
- **MEDIUM**: Non-critical feature failure, partial service disruption with an available workaround.
- **LOW**: Minor UI/cosmetic issues, general questions, or non-urgent tasks.
